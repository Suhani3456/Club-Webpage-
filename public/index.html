01About

A club for people who learn by making.

We started this club because lectures teach you how things work, and projects teach you why they break. Every semester we pick a few ideas, form small teams, and see them through to something people can actually use.

  • 01BuildTeam projects with code reviews and real deadlines.
  • 02TeachWeekly workshops led by members, not guest speakers.
  • 03CompeteHackathons and capture-the-flag nights, beginners welcome.
